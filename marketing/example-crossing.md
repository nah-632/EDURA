# EDURA — worked example

What the trigger produces. This is the answer to a bare `EDURA` message, written
exactly to the format in `gemini-edura-video-ad.md`. It exists so the output
shape is unambiguous before you spend a generation on it.

Angle used: **The crossing** — the dhow whose bow is a pen nib, writing a line of
light across the Gulf.

---

━━━ CONCEPT ━━━
A dhow crosses the bay before sunrise, and the line it leaves on the water lifts
off and becomes the Arabic tagline written in the air.

━━━ WHY THIS BEAT ━━━
For eight seconds the viewer watches a journey being drawn rather than described
— the feeling of setting out, which is what a parent looking for a tutor and a
graduate looking for students are both doing.

━━━ VEO PROMPT ━━━

`[0.0–2.5s]` Extreme close-up on black water, holding only one trembling
reflection of a distant light. A dhow's bow enters from frame left and cuts the
reflection in two. The bow is a pen nib — turquoise — and it is writing: a
single thin filament of light trails the hull across the surface. Camera: slow
lateral dolly at water level, 50mm, f/1.4, shallow enough that the far bay is
soft. Practically silent apart from water against wood.

`[2.5–5.5s]` The camera rises with the filament. It lifts off the water and
becomes a phrase of Arabic calligraphy suspended in the air — still wet, still
moving, edges catching the first light. Behind it, Manama's towers resolve out
of the blue hour; the east faces of the two tallest catch gold first. The dhow
is now small, crossing the lower third. Hold the calligraphy steady, centred,
breathing slightly with the camera.

`[5.5–8.0s]` The calligraphy settles down onto pearl-white paper, as though
laid by a hand just outside frame. The EDURA lockup — the dhow and pen nib —
lands beneath it. Everything is still. The water sound fades to one low note.

**Camera & lens:** 50mm throughout, f/1.4. No cuts — one continuous movement
from water level to eye level. Nothing spins, nothing flies.

**Light & grade:** pre-dawn blue lifted by the first low gold. Reflected
warmth in the water, cold in the sky, never crushed. Skin-free frame, so the
grade is entirely about surface: water, light, ink, paper.

**Palette notes:** navy `#16305A` in the pre-dawn sky and the towers, terracotta
`#BF6743` in the first light striking the calligraphy, sand `#D7C097` in the
hull and the sail, pearl `#FAF7F1` for the paper it lands on, logo turquoise
`#3F9DA5` for the pen nib and the wave beneath the mark.

**Audio:** no dialogue until the last beat. 0–5.5s: water against wood, one
distant gull, no music. At 5.2s a single oud note enters and holds. Voice-over,
Arabic, over the final two and a half seconds — a woman, unhurried, in a
Bahraini register: **"منصة بحرينية بفخر. طريقك إلى النجاح التعليمي."**

**On-screen text:** at 5.6s, centred, El Messiri, navy on pearl —
**طريقك إلى النجاح التعليمي**
at 6.4s, beneath it, smaller, Public Sans —
**Your Path to Educational Success**
Nothing else on screen. No logo animation, no swipe, no badge.

**Negative:** no drone orbit, no lens flare wash, no spinning text, no stock
"success" iconography, no grinning child, no graduation caps, no global-map
graphic, no numbers or statistics of any kind, no competing logos, no music
with a beat drop.

**VEO PROMPT — one paragraph, paste-ready:**

> A slow lateral dolly at water level across dark pre-dawn water, 50mm at f/1.4, where the only light is a trembling reflection of a distant lamp. A wooden dhow enters from the left and its bow — shaped as a fountain-pen nib, turquoise — cuts the reflection in two, trailing a single thin filament of light across the surface as if writing. The camera rises with that filament until it lifts off the water and becomes a phrase of Arabic calligraphy suspended in the air, still wet and moving, its edges catching the first low gold. Behind it, the towers of Manama in `#16305A` resolve out of the blue hour, their eastern faces warming to `#BF6743`; the dhow, sail in `#D7C097`, is small and crossing the lower third. The calligraphy settles onto pearl-white `#FAF7F1` paper and the EDURA lockup — the same dhow and pen nib, in `#3F9DA5` above a turquoise wave — lands beneath it and holds. Sound: water against wood and one distant gull, no music, until a single oud note enters and holds under a woman's unhurried Bahraini Arabic voice-over: "منصة بحرينية بفخر. طريقك إلى النجاح التعليمي." No cuts, no drone movement, no spinning text, no statistics.

## What this example deliberately does NOT do

Every one of these was available and was refused, because the platform cannot
back it:

* no "أكثر من ١٠٠٠ معلم" / "over 1,000 tutors"
* no "الأولى في البحرين" / "Bahrain's #1"
* no "٩٨٪ من أولياء الأمور راضون" / satisfaction percentage
* no testimonial from a parent or student
* no "معتمد من وزارة التربية" / Ministry-approved badge
* no number of lessons taught, families served, or years of operation

The launch reality — a new platform, a handful of tutors, a free first
subscription for the first fifteen — is the honest ground an ad can stand on.
Everything above works from that ground.

## Angle rotation

Next `EDURA` should not repeat this one. Remaining territory: **the desk at
sunset**, **the door**, **the click**, **the professional**, **the mark alone**,
**two languages one room**. The prompt keeps the list; it does not need you to.
